Title: Tajikfilm

Tajikfilm (Tajik: Тоҷикфилм , Russian: Таджикфильм ) is a Tajik (former Soviet) film studio. Tajikfilm was founded in 1930 as a newsreel studio, the studio released its first feature film in 1932; its first talky in 1935. In 1941 Tajikfilm merged with Soyuzdetfilm, only to reemerge in 1943. The studio produced films in both Russian and Tajik.
