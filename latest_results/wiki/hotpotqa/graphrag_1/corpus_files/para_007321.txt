Title: Yomil y el Dany

Yomil y el Dany is a Billboard Latin Music Award nominated Cuban duo composed of Roberto Hidalgo and Daniel Muñoz. They are among the pioneers of the music genre trapton, which is a new, evolved music style, stemming from reggaeton. This new sound combines hip hop, rap, electronic trap, dancehall, and elements of son music (traditional Cuban music) within its reggaeton base.
