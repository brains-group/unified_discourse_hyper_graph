Title: Barrie Dobson

Richard Barrie Dobson {'1': ", '2': ", '3': ", '4': "} (3 November 1931 – 29 March 2013) was a British historian who was a leading authority on the legend of Robin Hood as well as a scholar of ecclesiastical and Jewish history. He served as Professor of Medieval History at the University of Cambridge from 1988 to 1999.
