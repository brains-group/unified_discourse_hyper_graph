Title: Shannon Flynn

Shannon Flynn (born 22 August 1996) is an English actress from Rochdale, Greater Manchester, who is best known for playing Emily James in the BBC school-based drama, "Waterloo Road". Flynn has also had a presenting role on the CBBC show "Friday Download", and had starred in its film, "Up All Night". From 2013 to 2015, Flynn starred in the CBBC children's show, "Dani's Castle."
