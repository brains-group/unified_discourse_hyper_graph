Title: List of My Super D episodes

My Super D is a 2016 Philippine superhero fantasy drama television series directed by Frasco Mortiz and Lino Cayetano, starring Dominic Ochoa, Marco Masa and Bianca Manalo. The series premiered on ABS-CBN's "Primetime Bida" evening block and worldwide on The Filipino Channel on April 18, 2016 to July 15, 2016, replacing "Game ng Bayan". This is Dominic Ochoa's very first lead role after playing supporting roles in numerous teleseryes .
