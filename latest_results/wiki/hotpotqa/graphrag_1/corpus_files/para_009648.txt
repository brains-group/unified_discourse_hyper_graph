Title: Amen: The Awakening

Amen: The Awakening was a narrative driven first-person shooter/role-playing video game with some stealth elements by Cavedog Entertainment. It was to be the first FPS by Cavedog. 60% completed, it was canceled in 2000 for a variety of reasons, one of which being that its overall scope and proprietary game engine was far too ambitious, given computer hardware of the time.
