Title: 2017–18 EFL Cup

The 2017–18 EFL Cup is the 58th season of the EFL Cup. The competition is open to all 92 English and Welsh clubs participating in the Premier League and the English Football League. It is known as the Carabao Cup due to the start of a sponsorship deal with Carabao Energy Drinks after the tournament was unsponsored the previous year. The final will be held at Wembley Stadium in London. The reigning champions are Manchester United. The winners will qualify for the second qualifying round of the 2018–19 UEFA Europa League.
