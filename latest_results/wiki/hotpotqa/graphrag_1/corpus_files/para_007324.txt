Title: Música sertaneja

Música sertaneja (] ) or Sertanejo (] ) is a music style that had its origins in the countryside of Brazil in the 1920s. It is the most popular music style in Brazil, particularly throughout the southern/southeastern and center-western countryside Brazil. Subgenres include "sertanejo de raiz", "sertanejo romântico", and "sertanejo universitário".
