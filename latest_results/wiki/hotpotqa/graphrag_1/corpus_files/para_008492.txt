Title: Jang Kyung-jin

Jang Kyung-Jin (Korean: 장경진 ; born 31 August 1983) is a South Korean football defender who last played for Hong Kong First Division League side Kitchee. His previous clubs were Ulsan Hyundai Mipo, Chunnam Dragons, Gwangju Sangmu Phoenix, Incheon United, Gwangju FC and Oita Trinita in Japan.
