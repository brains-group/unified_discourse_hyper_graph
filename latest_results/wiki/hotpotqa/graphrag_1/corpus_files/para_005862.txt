Title: BBCU F.C.

BBCU Football Club, full name Big Bang Chula United Football Club, (Thai: สโมสรฟุตบอล บีบีซียู ) is a defunct Thai professional football club based in Bangkok, Thailand, owned by Montri Suwannoi. Founded as "Bangtoey Football Team" in 1976, the club changed its name many times, until finally, it became "BBCU" in 2011.
