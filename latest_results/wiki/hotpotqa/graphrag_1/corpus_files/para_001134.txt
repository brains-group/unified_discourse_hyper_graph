Title: Life, Death, Love and Freedom

Life, Death, Love and Freedom is the 20th folk rock album by singer-songwriter John Mellencamp and produced by T-Bone Burnett. It was released on July 15, 2008. At the end of 2008, Rolling Stone magazine named "Life, Death, Love and Freedom" No. 5 on its list of the 50 best albums of the year. The song "Troubled Land" was number 48 on "Rolling Stone"' s list of the 100 Best Singles of 2008. Antimusic.com named it number 18 on their top albums of the decade list. The album was named No. 38 in "Q"' s 50 Best Albums of the Year 2008.
