Title: Mireille Darc

Mireille Darc (] ; 15 May 1938 – 28 August 2017) was a French model and actress. She was Alain Delon's longtime co-star and companion. She appeared as a lead character in Jean-Luc Godard's 1967 film "Week End" (1967). Darc is a Chevalier of the Légion d'honneur and Commandeur of the Ordre national du Mérite.
