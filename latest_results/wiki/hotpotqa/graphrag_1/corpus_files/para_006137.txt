Title: Arnold Peder Møller

Arnold Peder Møller more known as A.P. Møller (2 October 1876 in Dragør – 12 June 1965 in Copenhagen, buried at Hellerup Cemetery. He was the fifth son out of nine and had three sisters as well. His father was Sea Captain Peter Mærsk Møller. And he became the father of Mærsk Mc-Kinney Møller.
