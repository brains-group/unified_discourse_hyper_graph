Title: Patrick Valéry

Patrick Valéry (born 3 July 1969) is a French footballer born in Brignoles who spent the majority of his playing career playing for AS Monaco. He also had spells with Toulouse FC and SC Bastia before joining Blackburn Rovers on a free transfer in June 1997. After an unsuccessful spell at Rovers he returned to France, re-joining Bastia in July 1998 for a fee of £80,000.
