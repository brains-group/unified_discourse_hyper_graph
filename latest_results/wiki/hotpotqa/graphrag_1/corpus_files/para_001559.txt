Title: Nick Franklin (baseball)

Nicholas Edward Franklin (born (1991--) 2, 1991 ) is an American professional baseball second baseman and outfielder for the Los Angeles Angels of Major League Baseball (MLB). He was drafted by the Seattle Mariners in the first round, 27th pick overall, of the 2009 Major League Baseball Draft. He attended Lake Brantley High School where he won numerous awards, including being named the player of the year by the "Orlando Sentinel" in 2009. Franklin made his professional debut in 2009, playing at two different levels in the Mariners' organization.
