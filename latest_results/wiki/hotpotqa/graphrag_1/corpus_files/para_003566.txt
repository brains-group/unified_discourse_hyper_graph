Title: David Thaxton

David Thaxton (born 12 May 1982) is a Welsh singer and musical theatre and opera performer. He starred in the Donmar Warehouse's "Passion", for which he won the 2011 Olivier Award for Best Actor in a Musical. He previously appeared as Enjolras in the West End production of "Les Misérables". In March 2011, he joined the cast of Love Never Dies, undertaking the role of Raoul de Chagny.
