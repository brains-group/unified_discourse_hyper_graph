Title: Régis Laguesse

Régis Laguesse (born Montreuil-Bellay 6 January 1950) is a French football coach and former player. He played for Angers SCO, SC Bastia and Stade Lavallois in the 1970s. He coached Thailand Premier League side BEC Tero Sasana in 2007, until he was replaced by another Frenchman Christophe Larrouilh. As of 2014, Laguesse was working as a coach in Congo's Katumbi Académie, a football academy owned by TP Mazembe owner Moïse Katumbi Chapwe.
