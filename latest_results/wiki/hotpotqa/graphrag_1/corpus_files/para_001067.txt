Title: 2006 Asian Games

The 2006 Asian Games (Arabic: دورة الألعاب الآسيوية 2006‎ ‎ , "Dawrat al-ʼAl‘ab al-Asīawīah"), officially known as the XV Asiad, was an Asian multi-sport event held in Doha, Qatar from December 1 to December 15, 2006 with 424 events in 39 sports and disciplines featured in the games. Doha was the first city in its region and only the second in West Asia (following Tehran in 1974) to host the games.
