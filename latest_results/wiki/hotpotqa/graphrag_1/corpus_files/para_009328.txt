Title: Alleghany Subdivision

The Alleghany Subdivision is a railroad line owned by CSX Transportation in the U.S. states of Virginia and West Virginia. It was formerly part of the CSX Huntington East Division. It become part of the CSX Florence Division on June 20, 2016. The line runs from Clifton Forge, Virginia to Hinton, West Virginia for a total of 78.6 miles. At its north end it continues south from the James River Subdivision and at its south end it continues south as the New River Subdivision.
