Title: Alexandra Park, Manchester

Alexandra Park is a 60 acre park in the Whalley Range district of Manchester, England. It was designed by Alexander Hennell, opened in 1868 and its initial purpose was to "deter the working men of Manchester from the alehouses during their day off". The lodge and gateways are the work of Alfred Darbyshire. The park was developed by the Manchester Corporation before the area was incorporated into the city, being sited wholly in the then Withington Local Board area. Work begun in late 2012 has led to major changes in the park.
