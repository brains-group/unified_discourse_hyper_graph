Title: Nova TV (Iceland)

Nova TV was a 24-hour music channel in Iceland. The channel launched in late 2007, playing non-stop music videos back-to-back. The channel first launched on Iceland's first purpose-built 3G mobile operator Nova. The channel is operated by 365 Corporation. The channel was available through digital television, exclusive to Nova mobile users and online at nova.is. The channel played a broad range of music genres from both local and international artists. The channel ceased operating in early 2014 and was replaced by new music channel Bravó.
