Title: 4th Impact

4th Impact (formerly known as The Cercado Sisters, The Cercados, The Gollayan Sisters, MICA, and 4th Power) is a Filipino girl group composed of sisters Almira, Irene, Mylene, and Celina Cercado. The group rose to popularity after competing in the 12th series of "X Factor UK", where they finished in fifth place. They are also known for having participated in several Philippine and international talent competitions, including the "World Championships of Performing Arts", where they won in 2006 and 2013, and the South Korean talent search "Superstar K6" in 2014, where they reached eighth place. The sisters were originally from Santiago City, Isabela, but are now based in Metro Manila, Philippines.
