Title: Bet I

"Bet I" is a song by American hip hop recording artist B.o.B, taken from his debut studio album "" (2010). The song, released April 20, 2010, as a promotional single from his debut album, features fellow Atlanta-based rappers T.I. and Playboy Tre. The song originally appeared on B.o.B's sixth mixtape "May 25th" (2010), minus a verse from T.I.
