Title: Predator (alien)

The Predator (also known as Yautja or Hish-Qu-Ten) is a fictional extraterrestrial species featured in the "Predator" science-fiction franchise, characterized by its trophy hunting of other species for sport. First introduced in 1987 as the main antagonist of the film "Predator", the Predator creatures returned in the sequels "Predator 2" (1990) and "Predators" (2010), the upcoming Shane Black installment "The Predator" (2018), and the crossover franchise "Alien vs. Predator" (2004) and "" (2007).
