Title: Diana Arbenina

Diana Sergeyevna Arbenina (née Kulachenko, Belarusian: Дзiяна Сяргееўна Кулачэнка ; Russian: Диа́на Серге́евна Арбе́нина , born July 8, 1974, Valozhyn, Minsk Oblast, Belarus SSR) is a Russian singer, musician, poet, leader of the rock group Nochniye Snaiperi. Honored Artist of the Chechen Republic (2005).
