Title: Cypriano de Soarez

Cypriano de Soarez, S.J. (1524–1593) was a sixteenth-century Spanish Jesuit who wrote "De Arte Rhetorica", the first Jesuit rhetoric textbook. Concerned that young students in Jesuit colleges were not ready for major rhetorical texts such as Aristotle's Rhetoric, Cicero's De Oratore, and Quintilian's Institutio Oratoria, Soarez wrote "De Arte Rhetorica" as digest of their work as an introduction for students still learning Latin.
