Title: Shahin Bushehr F.C.

Shahin Shahrdari Bushehr Football Club (Persian: باشگاه فوتبال شاهین شهرداری بوشهر‎ ‎ ) is an Iranian football club based in Bushehr, Iran. "Shahin" means "hawk" or "falcon" in Persian language. They bought the team license of Tarbiat Ilam to compete in the 2nd Division and hold home games at Shahid Beheshti Stadium.
