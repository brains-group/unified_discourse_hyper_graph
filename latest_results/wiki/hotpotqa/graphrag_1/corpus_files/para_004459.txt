Title: Patterson-UTI

Patterson-UTI Energy is an oil drilling company formed by the 2001 merger of Patterson Drilling (formed in 1979) and UTI Energy (formed in 1986 to purchase Universal Well Services, Union Supply Company, Triad Drilling Company, and International Petroleum Services Company from UGI Corp). The company operates land-based drilling rigs in the United States and Canada. The company comprises three segments: Contract Drilling, Pressure Pumping, and Oil and Natural Gas. It is a constituent of the S&P 400.
