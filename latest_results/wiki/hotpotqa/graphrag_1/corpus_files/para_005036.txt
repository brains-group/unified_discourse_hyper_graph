Title: Uruguay

Uruguay ( ; ] ), officially the Oriental Republic of Uruguay (Spanish: "República Oriental del Uruguay" ), is a sovereign state in the southeastern region of South America. It borders Argentina to its west and Brazil to its north and east, with the Río de la Plata (River of Silver) to the south and the Atlantic Ocean to the southeast.
