Title: Wild Things: Diamonds in the Rough

Wild Things: Diamonds in the Rough (also known as Wild Things 3) is a 2005 erotic thriller film directed by Jay Lowi and stars Sandra McCoy, Serah D'Laine, Linden Ashby, Dina Meyer and Brad Johnson. It is a sequel to "Wild Things 2" (2004) and the third film in the "Wild Things" series.
