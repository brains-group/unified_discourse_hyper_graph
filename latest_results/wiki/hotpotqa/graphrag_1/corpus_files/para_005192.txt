Title: Grigori 3

Grigori 3 is an American metal band formed in 1999 with Gwen Bartolini (vocals) and Ray Wise (guitar). Past members included Justin Bailey, Mark Feigl, and Mike Christopher. The current line-up includes Gwen Bartolini (vocals), Ray R Wise (guitar), Chris Beller (drums), Brain Bradbury (keyboard) and Mike Marcinkowski (bass). Their music is commonly compared to Evanescence, Collide and Lacuna Coil. Grigori 3 released their debut album, "Exile", in 2006 through Dark Star Records. Currently the band is back in the studio working on their third album with new drummer Chris Beller. New album should be released in 2017. The name Grigori relates to a race of fallen angels from mythology, and the number 3 represents "completion."
