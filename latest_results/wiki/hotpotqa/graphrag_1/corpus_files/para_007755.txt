Title: Juan Manuel Márquez vs. Joel Casamayor

Juan Manuel Márquez vs. Joel Casamayor was a boxing lightweight superfight. In 2008 reigning lightweight Champion "El Cepillo" Casamayor met former two-division World Champion Juan Manuel "Dinamita" Márquez at the MGM Grand Garden Arena in Las Vegas for a 12-round championship bout. Casamayor was the champion of boxing's lightweight division, recognized as the title holder by Ring Magazine, even though he didn't own any of the belts from boxing's four major sanctioning bodies.
