Title: Only You (2005 TV series)

Only You is a Korean television series produced and broadcast by SBS in early 2005. It is the only Korean drama shot in Italy. The city in which has been shot the drama in Italy is Vicenza which is located in the north East part, between Venice and Verona (about 50 km distant from Venice). It features Jo Hyun-jae (whose former agency, , is the program's production company) and Han Chae-young as the main protagonists. It was first aired in South Korea starting 4 June 2005.
