Title: Farmall M

The Farmall M, produced by International Harvester under the Farmall brand from 1939 to 1952, is a row crop tractor. The M was the largest of International Harvester's row crop tractors during its original production. It was the larger of the two prominent row crop tractors produced by IH from the late 1930s to the early 1950s, along with the Farmall H and its variants.
