Title: Janella Salvador

For her work, Salvador has won the Philippines Most Promising Female Star at GMMSF in 2014 for "Be Careful With My Heart" and has since garnered a FAMAS Award, three PMPC Awards (one for Best New Artist, two for acting) and holds three singles in OPM Top 10. Her self-titled debut album reached no. 1 nationwide shortly after release and attained platinum record status. She has also lent her voice to Disney for Hong Kong Disneyland and "Disney in Concert".
