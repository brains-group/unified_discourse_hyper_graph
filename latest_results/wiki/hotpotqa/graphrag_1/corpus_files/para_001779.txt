Title: Les Joueurs de football

Les Joueurs de football, also referred to as Football Players, is a 1912-13 painting by the French artist, theorist and writer Albert Gleizes. The work was exhibited at the Salon des Indépendants, Paris, March–May 1913 (no. 1293). September through December 1913 the painting was exhibited at Erster Deutscher Herbstsalon, Berlin (no. 147). The work was featured at Galeries Dalmau in Barcelona, 29 November – 12 December 1916 (no. 31), Gleizes' first one-person show. Stylistically Gleizes' "Football Players" exemplifies the principle of mobile perspective laid out in "Du "Cubisme"", written by himself and French painter Jean Metzinger. Guillaume Apollinaire wrote about "Les Joueurs de football" in an article titled "Le Salon des indépendants", published in L'Intransigeant, 18 mars 1913, and again in "A travers le Salon des indépendants", published in Montjoie! Numéro Spécial, 18 mars 1913.
