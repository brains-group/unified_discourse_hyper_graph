Title: 10 Years (2011 film)

10 Years is a 2011 American romantic comedy directed by Jamie Linden in his directorial debut. The film stars with an ensemble cast including Channing Tatum, Jenna Dewan, Justin Long, Kate Mara, Rosario Dawson, Oscar Isaac, Lynn Collins, Chris Pratt, Scott Porter, Brian Geraghty, Anthony Mackie, Kelly Noonan and Juliet Lopez. The film was released on September 14, 2012, in select theaters.
