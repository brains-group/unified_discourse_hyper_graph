Title: Blackpool South railway station

Blackpool South railway station serves the suburban south of the popular seaside resort of Blackpool, Lancashire, England. It is the terminus of the "South Fylde Line" 12+1/4 mi west of by rail, though most services run through from Colne. It lies only a short walk from Blackpool Football Club's stadium at Bloomfield Road. The station is managed by Northern, who operate all trains serving it. Blackpool South is located about 500 m from Waterloo Road tram stop on the Blackpool Tramway.
