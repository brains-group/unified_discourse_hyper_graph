Title: Bay St. Louis station

Bay St. Louis is a closed Amtrak intercity train station in Bay St. Louis, Mississippi, serving the "Sunset Limited". The Bay St. Louis station consists of two small platforms with indoor/outdoor shelters near the former Louisville & Nashville Railroad Depot, which was built in 1929, but is closed to the public. Service to this station has been suspended since Hurricane Katrina struck Bay St. Louis in 2005.
