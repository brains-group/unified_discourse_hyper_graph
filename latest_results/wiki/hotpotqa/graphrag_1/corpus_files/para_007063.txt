Title: Skip Peete

Skip Peete (born January 30, 1963) is an American football coach who serves as the current running backs coach for the Los Angeles Rams of the National Football League (NFL). Prior to the Rams, he coached running backs for the Chicago Bears, Dallas Cowboys, and Oakland Raiders. He also has experience in college, coaching running backs for two years at UCLA, wide receivers at Michigan State and Rutgers, and both running backs and wide receivers at the University of Pittsburgh.
