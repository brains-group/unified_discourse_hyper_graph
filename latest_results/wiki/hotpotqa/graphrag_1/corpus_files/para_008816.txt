Title: Randy Dickison

Randy Dickison is a high diver who has made attempts at the world record high dive. In 1982 he executed a 30 m (100 feet) dive and landed head first. He attempted 4 world records for highest dives from 51.5 m (169 feet), 51.8 m (170 feet), 52.4 m (172 feet), and 53 m (174 feet, 8 inches). He attempted to set the high diving world record in 1985 with a 53.2 meter (174 feet) dive at Ocean Park Hong Kong, but fractured multiple bones of his left leg in the attempt.
