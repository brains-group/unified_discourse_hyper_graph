Title: Shadows in Flight

Shadows in Flight is a science fiction novel by Orson Scott Card. When released in 2012, it became the tenth novel published in the "Ender's Game" series. The story follows on from where the original four "Shadow series" books left off. It is about Bean and his children discovering an ancient Formic "ark" during their journey in space. A sample chapter was released on November 28, 2011. The hardcover version was released on January 17, 2012, and the paperback was released on January 29, 2013. It was nominated for the Goodreads Choice Award for science fiction.
