Title: Bobby Black (journalist)

Bobby Black is senior editor of High Times magazine and author of the monthly column “Almost Infamous,” which covers the cannabis counter-culture entertainment scene. Bobby became senior editor of the quarterly Medical Marijuana News & Reviews in 2010. His involvement at High Times includes: writing feature articles (interviewed 2012 Presidential candidate Gary Johnson); creator and producer of the High Times magazine’s annual beauty pageant; host of the High Times Doobie Awards; annual contributor to the High Times Amsterdam Cannabis Cup and the High Times Medical Cannabis Cup. Bobby hosted the Sirius Satellite Radio Hard Attack channel's "Contact High" from 2004 to 2008, a show dedicated solely to stoner rock and metal. He was also credited as “Liner Notes, A&R” on “High Volume: The Stoner Rock Collection CD (High Time Records)”.
