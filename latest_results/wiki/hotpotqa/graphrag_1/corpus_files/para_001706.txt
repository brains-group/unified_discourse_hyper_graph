Title: Cara Blue Adams

Cara Blue Adams is an American author. She won "The Kenyon Review" Short Fiction Prize in 2008 and was first-runner up for the "Blue Mesa Review" Fiction Prize in 2010. Her work has appeared in many journals, including "The Kenyon Review", "Narrative Magazine" and "The Sun". She earned her MFA degree from the University of Arizona. From 2011 to 2013 she served as fiction and non-fiction editor of "The Southern Review".
