Title: Chilevisión

Chilevisión is the third oldest Chilean television network. Formerly called "Canal 9 de Televisión de la Universidad de Chile", "Teleonce (Universidad de Chile Televisión)" and "RTU (Red de Televisión de la Universidad de Chile"), this TV station was owned by Universidad de Chile, a Chilean state university. This educational institution sold a significant percentage of its TV channel to Venevisión, changing its name to "Chilevisión". It was later sold to "Claxson Interactive Group" and then to Chilean investor and ex-president Sebastián Piñera.
