Title: Cody B. Ware

Cody Morgan Verdecias (born September 4,1988), better known by his stage name Cody B. Ware is an American Rapper, Songwriter and A&R from Queens, New York who works on hip-hop and black metal. He is a member of the Queens-Based music collective World's Fair. Verdecias is a child of interracial parents and he is of half-Afro-Rican and half-Jewish descent. According to Verdecias, Bad Rabbits "blessed" him with the stage name Cody B. Ware after Retired Pro Wrestler Koko B. Ware. Verdecias says that growing up, his musical influences in hip-hop were Nas, Andre 3000, Big Pun, Khujo Goodie of Goodie Mob and he was also inspired by rock artists such as Daryl Palumbo of Glassjaw and Jacob Bannon of Converge.
