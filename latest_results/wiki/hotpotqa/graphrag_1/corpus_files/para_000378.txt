Title: Punarjani

Punarjani (English: "Reincarnation" ) is a 2003 Indian Malayalam drama film, directed by Major Ravi and Rajesh Amanakara, starring Pranav Mohanlal in the lead role. The film was produced by P. L. Thenappan under the production house, Sri Rajlakshmi Films. Pranav won the Kerala State Film Award for Best Child Artist for his performance in the film.
