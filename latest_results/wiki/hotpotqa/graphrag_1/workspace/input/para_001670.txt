Title: Mark Henry

Mark Henry made his video game debut in WWE SmackDown vs. Raw 2007. Ever since, he had appeared in WWE SmackDown vs. Raw 2008, WWE SmackDown vs. Raw 2009, WWE SmackDown vs. Raw 2010, WWE SmackDown vs. Raw 2011, WWE '12, WWE '13, WWE 2K14, WWE 2K15, WWE 2K16 (with 2 playable versions), WWE 2K17, and will appear as a "legend" in WWE 2K18
