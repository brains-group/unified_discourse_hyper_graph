Title: Rollins Air

Rollins Air was an airline charter company based in Honduras. It had Lockheed Tristars. In 2011 it was banned from operating in the European Union due to safety concerns. On 24 September 2012, its AOC (Air Operator's Certificate) was revoked and it subsequently expired. On 4 December 2012 (after the AOC expired) the airline was removed from EU list of banned air carriers.
