Title: Kamil Çörekçi

Kamil Ahmet Çörekçi (born 1 February 1992) is a UK born Turkish Cypriot footballer who plays as a right back for Trabzonspor in the Süper Lig. Born in London, Çörekçi attended Cateram High School and was a key member of the Redbridge District team Coached by Benn Goddard. This team lifted the London Youth Games football trophy in 2006 for the first time in 50 years also winning the fair play award.
