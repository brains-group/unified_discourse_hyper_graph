Title: Allavuddin Adbhuta Deepam

Allauddin Adhbhuta Deepam (English: Aladdin & Wonderful Lamp; Telugu: అల్లావుద్దీన్ అద్భుత దీపం ) is a 1957 Telugu fantasy film produced by M. Lakshmipathi on Jai Shakti Pictures banner, presented by T. S. Balaiah and directed by T. R. Raghunath. Starring Akkineni Nageswara Rao, Anjali Devi in the lead roles and music composed by Saluri Rajeswara Rao & Saluri Hanumantha Rao. The film is based on a story from "Arabian Nights" called "Aladdin & Magic Lamp" The film was simultaneously released in Tamil as "Allavudeenum Arputha Vilakkum" and in Hindi as "Alladdin Ka Chirag". This film is known to be the earliest Telugu film containing sequences in colour.
