Title: A. P. Møller School

The A. P. Møller School (Danish: "A. P. Møller Skolen" ) is a Danish gymnasium in Schleswig (Danish: "Slesvig"), Southern Schleswig, Germany. Inaugurated in September 2008, it was a gift to the Danish minority of Southern Schleswig from the A. P. Møller Foundation. It is named after the Danish shipping magnate of the same name.
