Title: Dolph Pulliam

Adolphus "Dolph" Pulliam (born March 14, 1946 in West Point, Mississippi) is a former American basketball player. He played collegiately at Drake University. He was named to Drake University's All-Century basketball team. He helped lead the Drake men's basketball team to the 1969 Final Four and a third-place finish. On 7 February 2009, Drake University retired Pulliam's #5 jersey that he wore for the Bulldogs.
