Title: Varvara Zelenskaya

Varvara Vladimirovna Zelenskaya (Russian: Варвара Владимировна Зеленская , often transliterated as Warwara Zelenskaja; born October 5, 1972 in Petropavlovsk-Kamchatsky) is a retired Russian alpine ski racer. She is the most successful Russian female World Cup ski racer, winning a total of 4 races (Svetlana Gladishiva is the only other Russian woman to win a World Cup race).
