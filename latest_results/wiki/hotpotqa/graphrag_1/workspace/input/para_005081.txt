Title: Hákarl

Kæstur hákarl (] ) (Icelandic for "fermented shark") is a national dish of Iceland consisting of a Greenland shark ("Somniosus microcephalus") or other sleeper shark which has been cured with a particular fermentation process and hung to dry for four to five months. Kæstur hákarl has a strong ammonia-rich smell and fishy taste.
