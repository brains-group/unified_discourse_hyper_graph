Title: Dude, That's My Ghost!

Dude, That's My Ghost! is a 2013 French/British animated television series produced by French production company Gaumont Animation that airs in English on Disney XD in the United Kingdom and BBC Kids in Canada. The series was created and designed by Jan Van Rijsselberge, creator of "X-DuckX" and "Robotboy". "Dude, That's My Ghost!" has been greenlit for 52 x 11 minute episodes. The show premiered on February 2, 2013 on Disney XD. The series has yet to air on Disney XD in the United States.
