Title: Eastern Shore Hospital Center

The Eastern Shore Hospital Center is an 76-bed psychiatric facility that is located in Cambridge, Maryland. It is owned and operated by the State of Maryland. Levels of care provided include acute and long-term inpatient psychiatric hospital services for adults aged 18 and older. The Center also operates an on-site 16-bed residential Assisted Living Program that is known as Stepping Stone at Manokin. The 40-bed Upper Shore Community Mental Health Center, a psychiatric hospital that was located in Chestertown, and the Eastern Shore Hospital Center were jointly administered for a number of years. The Upper Shore Community Mental Health Center was closed in March 2010.
