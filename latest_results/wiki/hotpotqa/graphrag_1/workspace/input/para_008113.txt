Title: Mistatim, Saskatchewan

Mistatim (2011 population 73) is a village in Bjorkdale Rural Municipality No. 426, Saskatchewan located just to the north of Sk Hwy 3. Mistatim is 35 mi east of the town of Tisdale and 40 mi west of Hudson Bay. The Greenwater Lake Provincial Park is 30 mi to the south. The village has its own village civic government within the rural municipal government of R.M. of Bjorkdale # 426
