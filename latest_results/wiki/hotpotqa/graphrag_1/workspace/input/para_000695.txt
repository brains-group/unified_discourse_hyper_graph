Title: 2017 Coastal Carolina Chanticleers football team

The 2017 Coastal Carolina Chanticleers football team represents Coastal Carolina University in the 2017 NCAA Division I FBS football season. The Chanticleers play their home games at the Brooks Stadium in Conway, South Carolina, and compete in the Sun Belt Conference. They are led by interim head coach Jamey Chadwell, who will also serve as offense coordinator, while permanent head coach Joe Moglia is on leave due to medical issues. This will be the Chanticleers' first year in the Sun Belt and the FBS, and their second of two years of their transition period. They will not be bowl-eligible until the 2018 season.
