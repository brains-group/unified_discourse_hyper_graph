Title: Holambi Kalan

Holambi Kalan is a suburban area in the Narela Sub Division of the North West Delhi district of Delhi, India. The area has a railway station named Holambi Kalan (Station Code: HUK). Its distance from Delhi Junction railway station is 21 km and it is 22 km away from New Delhi railway station. Holambi Kalan has been inhabited for over 400 years. Metro Vihar is a rehabilitated colony which is in Holambi Kalan. The population of Metro Vihar is 42,392 according to 2011 census. The area has developed considerably in the last 15 years, but still there are many civic problems concerning the area.
