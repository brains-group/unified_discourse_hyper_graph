Title: Iván Kamarás

Ivan Kamaras (born 22 December 1972) is a Hungarian actor who became first known worldwide for his role as Agent Steel in the 2008 superhero fantasy thriller "", directed by Guillermo del Toro. In January 2011, he played a Hungarian detective, Tibor Orban, in "Bloodlines", the fourth episode in the 14th series of the BBC crime drama "Silent Witness". In 2014 he played the role of Rasputin in the two-part History Channel miniseries Houdini. He lives in Santa Monica, California.
