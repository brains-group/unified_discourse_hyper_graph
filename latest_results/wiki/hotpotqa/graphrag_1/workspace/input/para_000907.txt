Title: Mira quién baila

¡Mira quién baila! (English: Look Who's Dancing! ) , formerly known as ¡Más que baile! (English: More Than Dancing! ) , is a Spanish reality television show, and part of the "Dancing with the Stars" franchise, in which a group of celebrities compete in a dancing contest of several styles over several weeks, partnered with dance professionals. The prize that the winner obtains is given to charitable organisations.
