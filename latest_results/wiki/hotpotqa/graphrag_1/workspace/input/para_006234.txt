Title: Fenwick, Northumberland

Fenwick is a small hamlet in the civil-parish of Stamfordham, Northumberland near Berwick upon Tweed and has a population of approx 400. Fenwick lies only three miles from Holy Island, Lindisfarne and the world famous heritage coastline. Fenwick lies alongside St Cuthberts Way, on which the monk St Cuthbert made his passage through Fenwick to the Holy Island. Fenwicks’ location meant it saw its fair share of skirmishes during the border raids from troublesome Scots. Its close proximity to Lindisfarne Castle, Bamburgh Castle and Chillingham Castle means it is an ideal base from which to explore the rich history of Northumberland and the Farne Islands.
