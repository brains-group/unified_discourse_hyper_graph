Title: Badger Mountain Ski Area

Badger Mountain Ski Area is a small ski area, 4 miles SW of Waterville, Washington. It is located on the northeastern flank of Badger Mountain. Established in 1939, it is currently a volunteer run ski area sponsored by the local Waterville Lions Club. The resort has a relatively low elevation, of 3145 ft , therefore the ski area does not usually receive adequate snow until January. The ski area has one small lodge, which has a small selection of rentals, and a kitchen.
