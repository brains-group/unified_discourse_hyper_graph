Title: Sleeper (band)

Sleeper are an English Britpop band, fronted by Louise Wener and formed in London. The group had eight UK Top 40 hit singles and three UK Top 10 albums during the 1990s Their music was also featured in the soundtrack of the pop cultural hit movie "Trainspotting". The band split up in 1998 but reunited in 2017 for some live appearances.
