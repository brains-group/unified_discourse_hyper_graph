Title: Addisu Legesse

Addisu Legesse is an Ethiopian politician. He was formerly the chairman of the Amhara National Democratic Movement, the Amhara region branch of the ruling EPRDF, a position from which he retired in 2010. He was also President of the Amhara region from 1992 until 2000, deputy prime Minister, and Minister of Agriculture and Rural Development until 2008. He then served as chairman of Ethiopian Airlines. He is currently head of the Meles Zenawi Academy, a quasi educational institution that's designed to train future EPRDF cadres in the ideology of Melesism, also known as, Ethiopian style Revolutionary Democracy
