Title: Emily Atack

Emily Jane Atack (born 18 December 1989 in Bedfordshire) is an English actress, best known for her role as Charlotte Hinchcliffe in the award winning E4 series "The Inbetweeners". In recent years she has starred films such as the remake of Dad's Army opposite Catherine Zita Jones and Bill Nighy and will co-star alongside Jennifer Saunders in Disney's live action picture, Patrick in 2018. She starred alongside Harvey Kietel and Gabriel Byrne in British film Lies We Tell. She has also starred in television programmes such as Rock and Chips (BBC), Little Crackers (Sky One), The Keith Lemon Sketch Show (ITV2) and The Tracey Ullman Show (BBC).
