Title: Chorlton-cum-Hardy

Chorlton-cum-Hardy is a suburban area of the city of Manchester, England, known locally as Chorlton. It is about four miles southwest of Manchester city centre. Chorlton contains the City of Manchester electoral ward of the same name, with a population taken at the 2011 census of 14,138. Chorlton Park is another ward in the same area. This ward had a separate population at the same census of 15,147.
