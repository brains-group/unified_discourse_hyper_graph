Title: Somebody's Gonna Love You

"Somebody's Gonna Love You" is a song written by Rafe VanHoy and Don Cook, and recorded by American country music artist Lee Greenwood. It was released in July 1983 as the second single and title track from the album "Somebody's Gonna Love You". The song was Lee Greenwood's sixth country hit and the first of his seven number ones on the country chart. The single went to number one for a week and spent a total of thirteen weeks on the country chart.
