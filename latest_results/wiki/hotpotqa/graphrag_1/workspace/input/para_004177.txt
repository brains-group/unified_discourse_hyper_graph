Title: Ilyas Abbadi

Ilyas Abbadi (born 21 October 1992 in Médéa) is an Algerian boxer. At the 2012 Summer Olympics, he competed in the Men's welterweight, but was defeated in the first round. At the 2016 Summer Olympics in Rio de Janeiro, he competed in the men's middleweight division. He was defeated in the second round by Zhanibek Alimkhanuly of Kazakhstan.
