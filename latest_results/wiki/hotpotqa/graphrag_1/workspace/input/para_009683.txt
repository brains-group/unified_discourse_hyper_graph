Title: Florida State Road 876

Daniels Parkway, originally known as State Road 876 (SR 876), runs from Tamiami Trail (U.S. Highway 41, US 41) in Fort Myers to SR 82 in Lehigh Acres. FDOT still maintains a small strip of road only slightly more than 1/2 mi long. SR 876 intersects Interstate 75 (I-75) and serves a nearby rest stop. Daniels Parkway is the main artery serving Southwest Florida International Airport.
