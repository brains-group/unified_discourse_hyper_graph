Title: Madala Ranga Rao

Madala Rangarao is an Indian Telugu Film Actor and producer noted for Erra Cinema or revolutionary movies in telugu film industry. He is popularly known as Red Star by people and associated with Communist Party of India and Prajanatya Mandali. Ranga Rao started his career with saitirical telugu film "Chairman Chelamaiah". He started his own banner Navataram Pictures and produced and acted movies like Yuvatharam Kadilindi (1980), Erra Mallelu (1981), Mahaprasthanam (1982), Praja Shakthi(1983), Veera Bhadrudu(1984). .
