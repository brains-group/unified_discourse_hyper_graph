Title: Easan

Easan (Tamil: ஈசன் ) is a 2010 Indian Tamil-language drama film written, directed and produced by M. Sasikumar, directing his second film after the blockbuster, "Subramaniapuram". It stars Samuthirakani, Vaibhav, producer A. L. Alagappan and Abhinaya in lead roles alongside several newcomers. The film was known and referred to as "Nagaram" and "Aaga Chiranthavan" before the official title was confirmed. It released on 17 December 2010.
