Title: Lilian Nalis

Lilian Bernard Pierre Nalis (born 29 September 1971) is a French former footballer who played as a midfielder. Born in Nogent-sur-Marne, he played in Ligue 1 for Caen, Guingamp, Le Havre and SC Bastia, Serie A for Chievo, the Premier League for Leicester City, and the Football League for Leicester, Sheffield United, Coventry City, Plymouth Argyle and Swindon Town.
