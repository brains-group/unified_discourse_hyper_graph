Title: Tony Almeida

Anthony "Tony" Almeida is a fictional character portrayed by Carlos Bernard on the television series "24". Almeida appeared in a total of 124 episodes (including ""), the third highest number of episodes of any character in the series behind Chloe O'Brian (125) and Jack Bauer (192), portrayed by Mary Lynn Rajskub and Kiefer Sutherland, respectively.
