Title: Geraldine Doyle

Geraldine Hoff Doyle (July 31, 1924 – December 26, 2010) has been widely promoted in the media as the possible real-life model for the World War II era "We Can Do It! " poster, later thought to be an embodiment of the iconic World War II character Rosie the Riveter. However, the 1942 news wire service photograph may depict another young war worker, Naomi Parker.
