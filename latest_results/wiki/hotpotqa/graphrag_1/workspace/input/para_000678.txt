Title: Marie Antier

Marie Antier (1687, Lyon – 1747, Paris), was a French opera singer (soprano). She was a student of Marthe Le Rochois and known for her roles in operas by Lully. She was a premier actress of the Academie de Musique (1720) and a court singer (1721). She played the leading roles of French opera from her debut in 1711 until her retirement in 1741.
