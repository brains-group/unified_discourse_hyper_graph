Title: Petar II Petrović-Njegoš

Petar II Petrović-Njegoš (, ] ; 13 November [O.S. 1 November] 1813  – 31 October [O.S. 19 October] 1851 ), commonly referred to simply as Njegoš, was a Prince-Bishop ("vladika") of Montenegro, poet and philosopher whose works are widely considered some of the most important in Montenegrin and Serbian literature.
