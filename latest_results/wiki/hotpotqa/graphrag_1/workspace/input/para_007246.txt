Title: George R. Dekle, Sr.

George Robert "Bob" Dekle, Sr. (born May 23, 1948) was an Assistant State Attorney in Florida's Third Judicial Circuit from 1975 through 2005, and the lead prosecuting attorney in the <nowiki>[</nowiki>1980 Orlando<nowiki>]</nowiki> murder trial of serial killer Ted Bundy which ultimately delivered the death penalty that was carried out in 1989. Dekle's book on the case, "The Last Murder: The Investigation, Prosecution, and Execution of Ted Bundy", was published in 2011.
