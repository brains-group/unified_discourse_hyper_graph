Title: Apodemia mormo

Apodemia mormo, the Mormon metalmark, is a species of metalmark butterfly (family Riodinidae). Its upperside is orange-brown to black, checkered with black and white spots. Its caterpillar host is various species of "Eriogonum" (wild buckwheat). Its adult food is nectar from the flowers of "Eriogonum" and other plants, especially yellow-flowered composites, such as "Senecio" and "Chrysothamnus" (rabbitbrush).
