Title: Männer haben kein Gehirn

Männer haben kein Gehirn (Men don't have Brains) is a promotional audiobook by German punk rock band Die Ärzte. It is a sampler of the 2001 "Lese-Tour" ("reading tour") "Die Ärzte - Hören, Sehen, Sagen ... Nicht! - Die Lesetour" featuring Markus Karg, the author of the official biography "Ein überdimensionales Meerschwein frisst die Erde auf". The original promo CD was only released for official fan club members, however, the audio files can still be downloaded from the band's official homepage.
