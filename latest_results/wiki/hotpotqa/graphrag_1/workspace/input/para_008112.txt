Title: Seronga

Seronga is a village in North-West District, Botswana. It is located close to beginning of the Okavango Delta, and has a local airstrip. The population of Seronga was 1,641 in 2001 census. In 2011 population census, the village had a population of 3716 making it the fourth largest village in Ngamiland West (Nokaneng to Gudigwa).
