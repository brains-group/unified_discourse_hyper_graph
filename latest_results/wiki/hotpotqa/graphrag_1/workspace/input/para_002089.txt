Title: Shillong Premier League

The Shillong Premier League is an association football league in India. It started with 8 teams in 2012. Royal Wahingdoh F.C. clinched the title for the third consecutive year after winning the previous two editions in 2010 and 2011. Royal Wahingdoh F.C. beat table toppers in the league stage, Rangdajied United F.C. comprehensively by 3-1 after the match was taken into extra time.
