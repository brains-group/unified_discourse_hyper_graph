Title: Akron Zips

The Akron Zips are the National Collegiate Athletic Association Division I athletic teams that represent the University of Akron , located in Akron, Ohio. United States. The football team competes in the Football Bowl Subdivision (FBS), the highest level for college football. Since 1992, the Zips have been members of the Mid-American Conference (MAC).
