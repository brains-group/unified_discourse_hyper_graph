Title: Glade (brand)

Glade (/gleɪd/) is an S. C. Johnson & Son brand of household air fresheners that were first introduced in 1956. The family of products include: Aerosol Sprays, Candles, Car Scented Oil, Carpet & Room, Glass Scents, PlugIns, PlugIns Scented Gel, PlugIns Scented Oil, Press'n Fresh, Secrets, Scented Oil Candles, and Wisp. Glade is a worldwide brand, known variously around the world as Glade, Gleid, Brise (Germany, France, and the Netherlands), etc. Brise was renamed in Glade in Germany, France, and the Netherlands in 2012.
