Title: Victory for the Comic Muse

Victory for the Comic Muse is the ninth studio album by The Divine Comedy. It was released by EMI on 19 June 2006. Neil Hannon did not choose the title as a reference to the group's 1990 debut "Fanfare for the Comic Muse". It's actually a quote from the book "A Room with a View" ("I have won a great victory for the comic muse").
