Title: American Housewife

American Housewife is an American sitcom television series that debuted on October 11, 2016 on ABC. Created and written by Sarah Dunn and co-executive produced with Aaron Kaplan, Kenny Schwartz, Rick Wiener, and Ruben Fleischer, the Kapital Entertainment–ABC Studios co-production. A preview was released on May 17, 2016. On November 4, 2016, ABC gave a full season order of 22 episodes with an additional episode ordered on December 13, 2016 for a 23 episode first season.
