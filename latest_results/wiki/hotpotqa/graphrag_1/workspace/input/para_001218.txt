Title: Witness for the Prosecution (1957 film)

Witness for the Prosecution is a 1957 American courtroom drama film with film noir elements co-adapted and directed by Billy Wilder and starring Tyrone Power (in his final screen role), Marlene Dietrich, and Charles Laughton. Set in the Old Bailey in London, the picture is based on the play of the same name by Agatha Christie and deals with the trial of a man accused of murder. Being the first film adaptation of this story, the movie features Elsa Lanchester in a supporting role and was adapted for the screen by Larry Marcus, Harry Kurnitz and Wilder.
