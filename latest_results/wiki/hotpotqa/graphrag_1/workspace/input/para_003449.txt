Title: Radioresepsjonen

Radioresepsjonen (Eng. "lit." "The radio reception") is a Norwegian radio show. From 2006 to 2013, it was broadcast on NRK P3, a radio station targeting the youth. Since 2014, it has been broadcast on NRK P13, which is aimed at a more adult audience than NRK P3. The show is hosted by brothers Steinar Sagen (host) and Tore Sagen (technician), and Bjarte Tjøstheim (sidekick). The show is very popular in Norway, and has developed cult status amongst a broad audience. The show is the most podcasted radio show in Norway, with over 200.000 downloads each month. Besides their radio show the trio has also had success with other live shows, TV-shows and podcasts.
