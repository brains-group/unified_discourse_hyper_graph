Title: Tehzeeb (2003 film)

Tehzeeb (English: "Etiquette") is a 2003 Indian drama film directed by Khalid Mohammed. It premiered on 21 November 2003. The film stars Shabana Azmi, Urmila Matondkar, Diya Mirza, Arjun Rampal and Rishi Kapoor in a special appearance. Urmila and Shabana were praised for their roles. It was inspired by Ingmar Bergman's Swedish drama "Autumn Sonata" (1978), and was dedicated to Bergman.
