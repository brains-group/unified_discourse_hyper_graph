Title: Hummingbird Air

Hummingbird Air is an airline offering scheduled and chartered air taxi services as well as cargo flights in the Caribbean. It is based at Henry E. Rohlsen Airport on the island of St. Croix, U.S. Virgin Islands. The airline was founded in late 2013 by Sam Raphael, a Dominican hotelier who wanted to improve air service to Dominica. As of May 2016, Hummingbird Air serves five destinations in the Caribbean with a fleet of three Beechcraft Model 99 aircraft.
