Title: Big Time Audition

"Big Time Audition" is the hour-long made-for-television pilot movie of the Nickelodeon television series, "Big Time Rush". It is the debut film of the series film franchise. It stars Kendall Schmidt, James Maslow, Carlos Pena Jr., Logan Henderson, and Stephen Kramer Glickman. It aired as a "sneak preview" on November 28, 2009 at 8:30 p.m. ET, following the iCarly special, "iMove Out". It is an hour-long special with the length of two regular episodes put together. The movie focuses on how four ordinary teenage friends from Minnesota get a chance to become the next chart-topping boy band, while going on an epic journey of a lifetime.
