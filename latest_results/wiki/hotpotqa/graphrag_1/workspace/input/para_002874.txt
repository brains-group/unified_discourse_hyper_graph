Title: Ruben Bagger

Ruben Bagger (born January 16, 1972) is a former Danish football player, who spent his entire professional career for Brøndby IF in the Danish Superliga, and played more than 300 matches for the club. He won five Danish Superliga championships and three Danish Cup trophys with Brøndby. Bagger played in the position of left winger or forward.
