Title: Paula Patton

Paula Maxine Patton (born December 5, 1975) is an American actress. Patton made her film debut in the 2005 comedy film "Hitch" and starred in the epic fantasy film "Warcraft" (2016), based on the game series of the same name. Patton has also been the female lead in "Déjà Vu" (2006), "" (2011) and "2 Guns" (2013), and appeared in the critically acclaimed "Precious" (2009).
