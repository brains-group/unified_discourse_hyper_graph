Title: Fine Art Showcase

Barry Chappell's Fine Art Showcase is a television show that sells signed original art, lithographs, silkscreens, etchings, sculptures and handmade glass from around the world. The show is part of Art and Coin TV's Thursday night line-up. It airs throughout the United States and Canada on DirectTV Channel 223 and online at www.ArtAndCoinTV.com.
