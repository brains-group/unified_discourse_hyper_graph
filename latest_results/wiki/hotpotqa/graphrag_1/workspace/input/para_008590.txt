Title: Ryan Ferguson (musician)

Ryan Ferguson (born  1975 ) is an indie rock guitarist and singer-songwriter, formerly of Southern California band No Knife. In 2004 he contributed the acoustic track "Wait for Me There" to the compilation album "San Diego Is Burning". Ferguson's solo debut "Three, Four" was released in July 2005, winning a San Diego Music Award for "Best Pop Record" that year. Critic Chris Nixon cited it as one of the best releases of the year for San Diego musicians. " The Sims 2" featured an interpretation of the album's lead track "Suddenly". He went on to tour with American rock band Switchfoot, and released a full-length album entitled "Only Trying to Help" in 2007.
