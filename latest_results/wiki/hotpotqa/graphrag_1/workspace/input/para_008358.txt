Title: 2013–14 Texas Longhorns women's basketball team

The 2013–14 Texas Longhorns women's basketball team will represent the University of Texas at Austin in the 2013–14 college basketball season. It will be head coach Karen Aston's second season at Texas. The Longhorns were members of the Big 12 Conference and will play their home games at the Frank Erwin Center. They finished the season with a record of 22–12 overall, 11–7 in Big 12 play for a tie for a third-place finish. They lost in the semifinals of the 2014 Big 12 Women's Basketball Tournament to West Virginia. They were invited to the 2014 NCAA Women's Division I Basketball Tournament which they defeated Penn in the first round before getting defeated by Maryland in the second round.
