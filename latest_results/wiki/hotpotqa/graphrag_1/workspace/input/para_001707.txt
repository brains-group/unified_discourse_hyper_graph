Title: Jason Gray (poet)

Jason Gray is an American poet whose first book, "Photographing Eden," was the winner of the Hollis Summers Prize from the Ohio University Press. Gray's poems have been published in "The American Poetry Review", "The Kenyon Review", "Poetry", and other prominent literary journals. He serves as co-editor of "Unsplendid", an online journal of formal poetry, and was the 2009 Peter Taylor Fellow at the Kenyon Review Writers' Workshop.
